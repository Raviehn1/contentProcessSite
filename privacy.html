Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled on the ContentProcess website and in connected platform integrations, including Pinterest.

2. Website access data

The hosting provider may process technical access information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, referrer and timestamps to deliver and secure the website.

3. Contact requests

Information voluntarily supplied in support requests is processed to answer the request and manage necessary follow-up.

4. Integration data

When ContentProcess is connected to a third-party service, it may process account identifiers, connection metadata, content assets, product metadata, workflow state and publishing information required for the user-configured workflow.

5. Pinterest

When connected to Pinterest, ContentProcess is intended to use the official Pinterest API and official OAuth authorization. Pinterest data is processed only as necessary to carry out user-authorized workflow actions.

6. Optional AI

AI-related input or output is processed only where the user enables the relevant feature. AI is not required for the core workflow concept.

7. Sharing and sale

Personal data is not sold. Data may be shared with technical providers or connected platforms only where required to provide requested functionality or meet legal obligations.

8. Retention and deletion

Data is retained only as long as necessary for the relevant purpose and applicable obligations. See the Data Deletion page for request instructions.

9. User rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability, as well as the right to contact a competent supervisory authority.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect legal, technical or operational changes.
