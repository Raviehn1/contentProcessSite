User rights

Data Deletion Request

Users may request deletion of data related to ContentProcess integrations.

How to request deletion

Contact the project operator through the Contact page and identify the relevant integration and account or workspace context.

What to include

  • a contact method,
  • the relevant third-party platform or integration,
  • the account or workspace context,
  • whether you request deletion, clarification or confirmation.

What happens next

The request will be reviewed and, where necessary, verified before relevant integration data is deleted in accordance with applicable obligations.

Third-party platforms

Some data may also exist directly on a connected platform. Platform-native data may need to be deleted through that provider as well.
